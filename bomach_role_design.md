**BOMACH OS --- ROLE FRAMEWORK & DESIGN SPECIFICATION (RFDS)**

**Overview**

This framework shall be attached to every Role and Designation within
Bomach OS.

It serves as the master blueprint that defines how every employee
operates within the organization.

The framework is the foundation for:

-   Human Resource Management

-   Organizational Structure

-   Performance Management

-   Task Management

-   Workflow Automation

-   Approval Management

-   Learning & Development

-   Succession Planning

-   Governance & Compliance

-   Reporting & Analytics

-   Access Control & Security

Every employee in Bomach OS must be assigned a Designation and a Role
Template.

The employee shall inherit responsibilities, permissions, workflows,
KPIs, OKRs, approval authority, training requirements, reporting
structures, and performance expectations from the assigned Role
Template.

This framework consists of twenty-four (24) core components that
collectively define the complete operational behavior of a role.

**OBJECTIVE**

The objective of this framework is to ensure that every role within
Bomach Group operates with:

-   Clear Purpose

-   Defined Responsibilities

-   Measurable Performance

-   Controlled Authority

-   Standardized Procedures

-   Continuous Development

-   Organizational Accountability

The framework eliminates ambiguity and creates a scalable operating
model that can be deployed across all Bomach Group subsidiaries,
branches, departments, and future business units.

It serves as the foundation for HR, Operations, Performance Management,
Training, Automation, Reporting, Promotions, Succession Planning, and
Access Control and it is listed from No's 1 - 24

1.  **[MISSION / PURPOSE]{.underline}**

-   **[DONE]{.underline}**

**Description**

Defines why the role exists and the value it contributes to the
organization.

**Objective**

Helps employees understand the primary outcome they are expected to
achieve.

**Example**

\*\*Marketing Manager\*\*

\"To generate qualified leads and increase revenue through effective
marketing strategies and campaigns.\"

**System Usage**

\* Displayed on role profile

\* Used during onboarding

\* Linked to KPIs and Targets
